# Procurement Specifications: Industrial Motor Housing

## Overview
We are looking to procure 5,000 units of Industrial Motor Housings for our upcoming production line.

## Mandatory Requirements
1. **Material**: Must be Aluminum Alloy A380.
2. **Certification**: Supplier must hold an active ISO 9001 certification.
3. **Lead Time**: Maximum lead time of 21 days from order placement.
4. **Tolerance**: Machining tolerance must be within +/- 0.05mm.

## Preferable (Non-Mandatory)
1. Sustainability report available.
2. ISO 14001 certification.
