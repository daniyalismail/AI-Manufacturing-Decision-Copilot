# Industrial Motor Procurement Requirements

## 1. Technical Requirements
- Minimum operating power: 50 kW
- Efficiency rating: > 92% (IE3 or higher)
- Maximum noise level: 75 dB
- Enclosure type: IP55 or higher for dust and water resistance
- Operating temperature range: -20°C to +50°C

## 2. Compliance and Certification
- Must possess ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality management.
- CE Marking required for European distribution.
- Environmental compliance with RoHS standards.

## 3. Commercial Constraints
- Maximum acceptable unit cost: $1,500
- Maximum lead time: 4 weeks (critical path requirement)
- Warranty period: Minimum 3 years
- Minimum volume guarantee: 5,000 units per annum

## 4. Operational Requirements
- Predictive maintenance sensor compatibility (Vibration and Temperature sensors).
- On-site training included for maintenance staff.
