# Procurement Request for Proposal (RFP)
Project: EV Motor Housing Procurement
Date: August 2026

## 1. Core Requirements
The buyer requires the following specifications for the procurement of EV motor housings:
- Material Specification: The housing must be manufactured using high-grade A380 Aluminum alloy. (MANDATORY)
- Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): The supplier must be able to support a maximum batch size of 1,000 units per order. (MANDATORY)
- Lead Time: The guaranteed lead time from order to delivery must not exceed 20 days. (MANDATORY)
